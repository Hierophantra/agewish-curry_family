    **Step 1 — Typography (any page, desktop):**
    - Confirm headings render in Cormorant Garamond (narrow, elegant, high contrast vs. Georgia)
    - Open DevTools → Elements → inspect an h1 or h2 → computed font-family should show Cormorant Garamond
    - Confirm no heading looks "too tight" or "too loose" — if spacing feels off, note it for tuning

    - No horizontal scrollbar on the page body

    **Step 4 — Photographs at 375px:**
    - Visit http://localhost:3000/photographs
    - Photo grid shows 1 column at 375px
    - No horizontal overflow on the page

    **Step 5 — Films at 375px:**
    - Visit http://localhost:3000/films
    - Video grid shows 1 column at 375px
    - Video player aspect ratio maintained; no overflow

    **Step 6 — Family tree at 375px:**
    - Visit http://localhost:3000/tree
    - Tree canvas: can scroll horizontally (swipe or drag scrollbar)
    - Right-edge gradient indicator visible (white fade on right side of tree container)

    - PersonPanel max-height ~60% of viewport; can scroll within the panel if content is long
    - Close button (×) in panel closes it
    - TopNav tabs: all 4 tabs visible/scrollable, no overflow outside nav bar

    - Home page: count exactly 3 star instances (TopNav, Hero, Footer)

    - No element should show 600, 700, or 800 weight
